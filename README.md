Hello, I live in Astoria, NY. When I walk to school, I pass by many houses, stores, and restaurants and I often see a lot of garbage outside the buildings to be picked up. This is so much waste! This made me wonder if some of this garbage could be re-used by someone else. So, what if we could make Astoria's businesses work like an ecosystem, where someone’s waste becomes the other’s resource?
 
For example, grocery stores sometimes throw away food that could be donated before it expires. Restaurants throw away leftover food that can be used for soil and gardening. Stores throw out boxes that can be used by moving companies. Coffee shops throw out coffee grounds that can be used as compost by community gardens. Construction companies throw out leftover wood that can be used by carpenters or schools for student projects. 

This gave me the idea of creating this app and call it Astoria Circular Exchange. Instead of waiting for something to become garbage and pollute the environment, this app will try to find another local business that can use it. So, let me show you how it works.
 
Astoria Circular Exchange is an app that helps local businesses share and reuse materials instead of throwing them away. The goal is to reduce waste, save resources, and help the Astoria community.

### How Does Matching Work?
Businesses create an account online and enter four important things: the materials they want or have to give away, the amount, the pickup time, and their address. The address is used to calculate the distance between businesses.
The app uses these four things to calculate a score: 40 points for material fit, 20 for distance, 20 for amount, and 20 for pickup time. The high score shows a good match. Then, the app sorts the matches from highest to lowest score. Businesses can see their matches and the points for each category. Businesses can choose a match and arrange to pick up the materials. This way, the app brings together businesses to share and reuse materials instead of throwing them away. 

Careful: If no matches appear, check that all fields are complete. You may need another business with compatible materials, a nearby address, and a suitable pickup time. There is an example.
