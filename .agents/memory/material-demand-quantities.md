---
name: Material demand quantities
description: Product rule for recording supply and requested amounts and using them in exchange matching.
---

Each business records an amount alongside the material it offers and an amount alongside the material it wants. Amount fit compares a compatible partner's offered quantity with the quantity requested.

**Why:** the user clarified that supply and demand are both material-and-amount pairs, and that amount fit should contribute meaningfully to matching.

**How to apply:** Keep each amount adjacent to its material in the business entry flow. Compare amounts only when their units and time periods can be meaningfully normalized; missing or incomparable values should not be treated as a failed match.
